Generate content for any platform using your built-in LLM. Do not call external APIs.

## Content Types

- **Post**: Short caption + hashtags + CTA (2-3 sentences)
- **Thread**: 3-5 connected posts
- **Reel script**: Scene descriptions + audio cues
- **Carousel**: Slide titles + key points

## Style

Brand: Technical but accessible software developer. 
- Keep posts under 200 words
- Add 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Include a call to action
- Use emojis naturally (not excessive)
